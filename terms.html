Terms and Content Policy

The rules for using IRLA, and what is not allowed on it.

Last updated 26 August 2026

What IRLA is

IRLA lets you set goals you can count, log progress against them, and share that with friends who can vouch for it. You need an account. You must be 13 or older to use it.

Your content stays yours

You keep ownership of everything you post — your goals, progress notes, photos and comments. You give us permission to store and display it inside the app so the app can work: showing your posts to the friends you shared them with, and showing your profile according to the visibility you chose.

We do not sell your content, use it to train anything, or show it to people you did not share it with.

What is not allowed

Do not post, share, or send:

  • Nudity or sexual content. Including sexualised images of anyone.
  • Anything sexual involving a minor. This is reported to the authorities, always, with no exception and no warning.
  • Graphic violence — real-world violence, gore, or injury, outside a genuine news context.
  • Harassment or bullying — targeting, threatening, or humiliating a person.
  • Hate speech — attacking people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, disability, sex, gender identity, or sexual orientation.
  • Content promoting self-harm, suicide, or eating disorders.
  • Spam, scams, or misleading content — including fake goals posted to advertise something.
  • Illegal activity, or content that helps someone commit a crime.
  • Someone else's private information — address, phone number, documents — without their permission.
  • Impersonation of another person or organisation.

Do not attempt to break into the service, other people's accounts, or the data behind either.

Reporting and blocking

To report something: open the post and tap Report this post, long-press a comment to report it, or open a profile and tap Report. Pick a reason and send.

To stop seeing someone: open their profile and tap Block. They disappear from your feed and cannot interact with you. Blocking and reporting are separate — you can do either or both.

Reports are reviewed by a person. The account you report is not told who reported it.

You can also email ie002.irla@gmail.com if you would rather not use the in-app route, or if the content is urgent.

What we do about it

When something breaks these rules we may remove it, limit what an account can do, or terminate the account. Serious cases — anything involving a minor, a credible threat of violence, or an immediate risk to someone's life — are acted on immediately and may be referred to the authorities.

We aim to review reports promptly. IRLA is a small project, not a company with a moderation department, and we would rather say that plainly than imply a response time we cannot keep.

Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time — see deleting your account. We may suspend or terminate an account that repeatedly breaks these rules, or once, if the breach is serious enough.

No warranty

IRLA is provided as it is, without any guarantee that it will be available, error-free, or that your data will never be lost. Export your data if it matters to you — Settings → export your data gives you a complete copy.

Changes to these terms

We may update this page. If a change materially affects your rights we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to use IRLA after a change means you accept it.
