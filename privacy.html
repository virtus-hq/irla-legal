Privacy Policy

IRLA — In Real Life Achievements

Last updated: 21 August 2026

IRLA lets you set challenges you can count, log your progress, and share that with friends who can vouch for it. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and how to get rid of it.

We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

We do not collect your location, contacts, calendar, microphone, or any advertising identifier.

Camera and photos

You can attach a photo to a progress entry, either from your photo library or by taking one. The app asks for camera permission only when you choose to take a photo. Photos from your library go through the system photo picker, which gives the app access only to the specific image you select — never your whole library.

A photo you attach is uploaded to our servers and shown alongside that entry to whoever can see it. Deleting the entry, or your account, deletes the photo.

Who else sees your data

Other users. What they can see depends on your profile visibility setting (public, friends only, or private) and on whether a given challenge is public. Someone you block cannot see your profile, posts, or challenges.

Service providers. We use a small number of third parties to run the app:

Steam, Xbox (via OpenXBL), PlayStation Reads your public profile and milestones to import them. Only if you link that account

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything you created is removed — challenges, progress entries, photos, posts, comments, reactions, friendships and linked accounts. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Your rights

You can, at any time and without contacting us:

  • Export everything. Settings → your data, gives you a complete JSON copy of everything we hold.
  • Delete your account. Settings → delete account, or use the account deletion page.
  • Change your visibility. Settings lets you make your profile public, friends-only, or private.

If you are in the EU or UK, these cover your GDPR rights to access, erasure and portability. If you would rather we did it for you, email us.

Children

IRLA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details, government identifiers, or precise location — there is very little here that would be valuable to an attacker.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that materially affects you, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
