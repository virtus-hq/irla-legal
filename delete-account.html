Delete your IRLA account

Two ways to do it. Both delete the same things, permanently.

In the app

  1. Open IRLA and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is gone immediately.

If you would like a copy of your data first, Settings → export your data gives you a complete JSON file. Do that before deleting — afterwards there is nothing left to export.

By email

If you cannot get into the app — you have lost your password, or already uninstalled it — email us from the address on the account:

REPLACE_WITH_CONTACT_EMAIL

Include the username or email on the account. We will confirm the request, delete it within 30 days, and email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account, immediately and permanently:

  • Your profile — email, username, display name, bio, photo
  • Every challenge, stage and progress entry you created
  • Photos you attached to progress entries
  • Your posts, comments and reactions
  • Your friendships, friend requests and blocks
  • Your achievements and any verifications on them
  • Any linked Steam, Xbox or PlayStation account
  • Your notification and device registrations

What does not get deleted

  • Comments other people wrote — those are theirs, not yours, and stay on the posts they were written on. Your name is removed from anything that referenced you.
  • Server logs, which are kept for a short period for security and troubleshooting, and are not tied to your profile once it is gone.

There is no recovery

Deleting is immediate and cannot be reversed. We do not keep a copy, so we cannot restore an account after the fact — not even the same day. If you are unsure, export your data first, or set your profile to private instead: Settings → profile visibility → private hides everything from everyone while keeping the account.
